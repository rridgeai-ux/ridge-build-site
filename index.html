About us

Built to stand the test of time.

Ridge Build was formed in 2025 to fill a gap. Large building groups carry heavy overheads and lose the personal touch. Small companies often don't have the systems and processes to finish a build on time and on budget, and to still be around in years to come. We set out to offer both: a personal service, backed by the systems to deliver.

We don't exist to be the biggest company. We exist because we love building. We want to give our clients a home that hasn't just been built to satisfy the contract, but built to deliver the home they set out to have. And our partnership doesn't stop at completion. We stay with you through follow-up care for years to come.
